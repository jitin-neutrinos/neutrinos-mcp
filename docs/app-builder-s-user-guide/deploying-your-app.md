# Deploying your App

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!app-builder-s-user-guide/deploying-your-app>

![](/resources/Storage/app-builder-s-user-guide/deploy.png)

Neutrinos Platform allows you to deploy your app on Cloud and On-premise. You can choose the delivery platform for example - Windows, Android, and iOS while deploying your app.

Follow these best practices while deploying your app:

- Always [initialize your app](/articles/app-builder-s-user-guide/initialize-web-app) before deploying.
- [Create custom tasks](/articles/app-builder-s-user-guide/create-custom-tasks) to execute the commonly used tasks.
- Instead of deploying your app directly, choose to [Live Preview](/articles/app-builder-s-user-guide/live-preview-web-app)your app, validate the look and feel of your app, and then deploy your app.
