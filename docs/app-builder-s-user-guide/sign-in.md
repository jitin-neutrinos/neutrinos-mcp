# Sign Up

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!app-builder-s-user-guide/sign-in>

If you are a new user and want to sign-up for a free trial, navigate to the [Neutrinos website](https://www.neutrinos.co/) and click the [Free Trial](https://www.neutrinos.co/free-trial/) link.

In the free trial form, enter the required details and click **Submit. **By clicking the **Submit **button, you accept to be reached out by the Neutrinos team to process your request.

After this, a new trial account will be set up for your organization to use. Also, you will be granted the organisation admins privileges. See [Free Trial Terms](/articles/concepts-publication/free-trial-terms) to learn more about the trial account.

If you are an existing user, sign in to the [Neutrinos Console](https://console.neutrinos.co/) with your username and password to access your organization's workspace, download the development environment, and start developing applications.
