## Image Carousel

### Overview

The** Image Carousel** template is used to show images in a cyclic slideshow. It works with a series of images, images, with captions. It also includes support for previous/next controls and indicators. This template is responsive so it will display gorgeously on all screen types including mobiles and computers. Also, our carousel will automatically change its size to fit the image’s size.

This template has Neutrinos IDS OAuth Strategy enabled. See [Configure IDS](/smart/project-sample-how-to-guide/configure-your-ids) to learn more.

### Usage

Carousels are often used to display a series of looping images. There are some other popular usages of the carousel. Some of them are:

- Teasers

- Articles

- Entire sections of web pages

### How to Use

1. In the Neutrinos Store, open the **Image Carousel** template.
2. Click the **Download** icon and select **Open Neutrinos Studio** in the pop-up that appears and you will be navigated to the Neutrinos Studio.
3. In the Neutrinos Studio, click **Yes** on the confirmation pop-up and the template gets installed.
4. Create a new app using the image-carousel template and enter the metadata for your app in the **ENTER APP DETAILS** window. Click **CREATE** and the app gets created.
5. Open the created app and navigate to the** Settings** menu. By default, the Neutrinos IDS OAuth Strategy is selected. Enter the **Application display name** and click **Save. **
6. You will see a pop-up to confirm registering for the IDS. Click **Okay** and the IDS flows are generated.
7. Navigate to the server services editor and confirm whether the IDS flows are generated. ![server services of the template](/resources/Storage/components-guide-8/serverservicesTEMP.png)
8. If the flows are not generated, navigate back to the IDS settings editor and click the **Regenerate IDS Flows **button.
9. After confirming, **Initialize** your app.
10. After initialization is completed. Select **Live View** and run the task.
11. The template is rendered on the browser. Login to the template and the template looks like this.

![First page of the template](/resources/Storage/components-guide-8/ICF1.png)

![Final template page](/resources/Storage/components-guide-8/ICF2.png)

You can further design the template as per your needs in the application.
