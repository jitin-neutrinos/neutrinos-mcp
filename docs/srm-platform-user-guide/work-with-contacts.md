# Contact List Page

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-contacts>

In this section of the document, you will learn how to create and manage [contacts](/articles/project-user-guide-srm/contact) on the SRM platform.

You create and manage contacts on the **Contact List **page. Click **Contacts** on the [Module navigation panel](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/srm-platform-ui/a/h4_1300478116) to access this page.

### Contact List Page

The contact list page displays all the contacts that you and your reportees own.

You can perform the following actions on this page:

- [Add label](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-labels)
- [Re-assign contacts](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/re-assign-imported-contacts)
- [Export contacts as CSV](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/export-as-csv)
- [Perform bulk actions](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/perform-bulk-actions)
- [Search contacts](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/search-contacts-and-deals)
- [Filter contacts](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/filter-contacts-and-deals)

This page consists of two tabs:

- My Contacts
- Team Contacts

#### My Contacts

The **My Contacts** tab displays all the qualified leads that you own. This tab enables you to view the contact details such as name, mobile number, email, and last updated date.

For each contact, you can click on respective icons to perform the following actions:

- [Call](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/place-a-call)
- [Add Task](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-tasks)
- [Add Appointment](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-appointments)

#### Team Contacts

| ![Information](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/info.png) | You cannot view the **Team Contacts** tab if you have no reportees under you. |
| --- | --- |

The **Team Contacts** tab displays all the contacts that are added by your reportees. This tab displays details such as name, mobile number, email, last updated date, and the name of the reportee (contact owner) to whom this contact belongs. For example, these are the contacts of Aayush Khatri.
