# Add Appointment

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-appointments>

### Add Appointment

You can add an appointment by clicking the **Add Appointment** icon on any of the Contact and Deal pages. Or, by clicking the **Add **button on the [action panel](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/srm-platform-ui/a/h4_898368986) and selecting **Appointment**.

On the **Add Appointment **page, enter the following details and click the **Add** button:**Title: **The title for the appointment. This is a mandatory field.**Related Contact**: The contact name that is related to this appointment. This field will be prefilled if you are adding the appointment from a particular contact or deal details page.**Add Participant: **Click the **Add Participant **button and enter the additional participant for this appointment. **Related Deal:** The deal name that is related to this appointment. This field will be prefilled if you are adding the appointment from a particular contact or deal details page.**Location: **The address of the location at which the appointment is going to take place. **Description: **The description for the appointment.**Date&Time**: The from and to date&time for the appointment. **Add Video Conferencing: **Click this button to add Video Conferencing for the appointment.![add appointment](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/add_appointment.png)

### View Appointment

You can view the created appointments from the **Daily Summary** Page or using the **Calendar** from the action panel.

To view the appointments from the Daily Summary page, perform the following:

1. Open the Daily Summary page.
2. Select the appointment that you want to view from the Today's Appointments or Future Appointments card.
3. The View Appointment page appears on the right.

To view the appointments from the calendar, perform the following:

1. Click the Calendar icon on the action panel.
2. Navigate to the day tab to view the appointments for the day. You can also select the Week and Month tabs to view the future appointments.
3. Click on the appointment and the View Appointment page appears to the right.

To view the appointments from the contact details page, perform the following:

1. Open the contact details page of the contact.
2. Navigate to the **All Activities **section, and select** Appointments.** You can view all the appointments scheduled to this contact.

### Edit Appointment

To edit an appointment, perform the following steps:

Open the appointment from the **Daily Summary** Page or the **Calendar** from the action panel. The **View Appointment** page is displayed to the right.Click the **Edit **icon and make changes. Click update and the changes are saved.![edit appointment page](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/edit_appointment.png)

### Delete Appointment

To delete an appointment, perform the following steps:

Open the appointment from the **Daily Summary** Page or the **Calendar** from the action panel. The **View Appointment** page is displayed to the right.Click the **D****elete **icon. A pop-up appears to confirm the deletion. Click **Yes **and the appointment gets deleted. ![delete icon for the appointment](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/delete%20appointment.png)
