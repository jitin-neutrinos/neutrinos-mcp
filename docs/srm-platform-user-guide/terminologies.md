#### FNA

Financial Need Analysis (FNA) is a no-cost, no-obligation service design to determine the insurance needs of a client.

The purpose of an FNA is to understand the current and future state of a client's financial needs. This is to ensure that a product is not sold without an overall assessment of the clients existing financial position.
