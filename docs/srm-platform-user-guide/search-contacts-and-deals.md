# Search Contact

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/search-contacts-and-deals>

### Search Contact

To search a Contact:

1. On the [Contacts List](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/srm-platform-ui/a/h4_1409235390) page, navigate to the **My Contacts** tab to search for your contacts, or the **Team Contacts **tab to search for contacts added by your team.
2. Click the** search **icon and search for the contact based on the contact name, phone number, or email id.

### Search Deal

To search for a Deal:

1. On the Deals List page, navigate to the** My Deals** tab to search for your deal, or the **Team Deals** tab to search for deals added by your team.
2. Click the** search **icon and search for the deal based on the deal name, phone number, or email id.
