# Add Stage

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/manage-deal-pipeline>

The deal pipeline represents the sales cycle that the agents or insurers of your organization follow in the process of selling a product to the clients.

This section allows you to configure stages for the deal pipeline depending on the sales cycle that your organization follows. By default, the first and last stages are defined. The first stage is the **New Lead** stage which can be renamed according to the organization's requirement. The last stage is the **Won or Lost **stage which cannot be updated.

On this page, you can:

- [Add Stage](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/manage-deal-pipeline/a/h3__680626735)
- [Edit Stage](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/manage-deal-pipeline/a/h3__261829340)
- [Delete Stage](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/manage-deal-pipeline/a/h3_1048610493)
- [Re-order Stages](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/manage-deal-pipeline/a/h3_567365171)

### Add Stage

To add a stage:

On the **Settings **page, select **Deal Pipeline.**On the **Configure Deal Pipeline **page, click the **Add Stage **button**. **Enter the name of the stage in the **Enter Stage Name** field and click the **Save Label** icon or press **Enter**. ![Add stage for the deal pipeline](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/Add_stage.png)

### Edit Stage

To edit a stage name:

On the **Settings **page, select **Deal Pipeline.**On the **Configure Deal Pipeline **page, click the **Edit Label **icon next to the stage that you want to edit.Edit the name of the stage and click the **Save Label** icon or press **Enter**.![Edit stage for the deal pipeline](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/edit_stage.png)

### Delete Stage

To delete a stage:

1. On the **Settings **page, select **Deal Pipeline.**
2. On the **Configure Deal Pipeline **page, click the **Delete **icon next to the stage that you want to delete.

### Re-order Stages

To re-arrange the order of the pipeline:

1. On the **Settings **page, select **Deal Pipeline****.**
2. Click **Feedback Outcome**.
3. On the **Configure Deal Pipeline **page, click and hold the **Rearrange icon** next to the stage you want to move.
4. Drag upwards or downwards to re-arrange and the order of the stages gets updated.
