# Delete a Contact

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/delete-a-contact>

To delete an existing contact, perform the following steps:

Open the contact that you want to delete.


 Click the **Delete **icon on the contact header. A pop-up appears to confirm your action.

 ![delet icon for the contacts](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/deleteicon_contacts.png)

 Click **Yes **and the contact gets deleted.


 ![delte pop-up](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/delete-pop-up.png)
