# Configure Theme and Logo

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/configure-themes-and-logos>

You configure the platform theme and add a logo using the **Settings** > **Theme and Logos **page.

A logo is an image that visually identifies your company. It can contain the company's name or just a visual representation.

To add a logo to the platform:

1. Upload or drag and drop your organization's logo on the **Theme and Logo page.**
2. Click **Save **and the **logo** gets added.

The theme represents the overall appearance of your application. In essence, your application theme is a direct representation of your brand and has a direct impact on your users' experience.

To set the theme of the platform:

1. Select your organization's **Primary Color Code **and **Secondary Color Code **on the** Theme and Logo **page.
2. Click **Save**.
