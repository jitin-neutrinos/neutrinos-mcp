# Place a call

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/place-a-call>

To place a call:

- Click the **Call **icon.
- A Call Directory pop-up appears from where you can select the phone number to place a call.
- Select the phone number and click the **Call **button. You can select only one number at a time.
- When the call is placed, you will receive a call on your registered mobile number. Once you answer the call, your contact will receive the call.
- As soon as your contact picks up the call, the call recording will be played that is configured in the [Configure Voice Call Recording Message](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/manage-voice-call-recording-message) section by the admin. After which, you can converse with the contact.
- After your call ends, the recording of the conversation will be available in the [Call Activities](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/manage-contacts/a/h3_1475553155) section.
