# Deal List Page

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-deals>

In this section of the document, you will learn how to create and manage [deals](/articles/project-user-guide-srm/deal)on the SRM platform.

You create and manage deals on the **Deals List **page. Click **Deals **on the [Module navigation panel](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/srm-platform-ui/a/h4_1300478116) to access this page.

### Deal List Page

The deal list page displays all the deals that are associated with you and your reportees.

You can perform the following actions on this page:

- [Export deals as CSV](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/export-as-csv)
- [Perform bulk actions](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/perform-bulk-actions)
- [Search deals](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/search-contacts-and-deals)
- [Filter deals](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/filter-contacts-and-deals)

This page consists of two tabs:

- My Deals
- Team Deals

#### My Deals

The **My Deals **tab displays all the deals that are associated with you. This tab enables you to view details such as deal name, contact name, value, stage, and last updated date.

For each deal, you can click on respective icons to perform the following actions:

- [Call](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/place-a-call)
- [Add Task](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-tasks)
- [Add Appointment](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-appointments)

#### Team Deals

| ![Information](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/info.png) | You cannot view the **Team Deals** tab if you have no reportees under you. |
| --- | --- |

The **Team Deals** tab displays all the deals that are added by your reportees. This tab displays details such as deal name, contact name, value, stage, and the name of the reportee (deal owner) to whom the deal belongs.
