# Contact Details Page

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/manage-contacts>

### Contact Details Page

When you [add a contact](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/create-a-contact) or click on an existing contact, you are navigated to the **Contact Details** page. The contacts details page allows you to perform different sales activities.

It includes the following sections:

- Contacts Header
- Contact Summary
- All Activities which includes:
  - Activities
  - Deals
  - Documents
- Notes

### Contacts Header

This section displays a few details of the contact such as contact name, labels (if any), [contact owner](/articles/project-user-guide-srm/contact-owner), and contact ID. It also provides icons to [call](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/place-a-call), [add label](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-labels), and [delete](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/delete-a-contact) the contact.

---

### Contact Summary

This section displays the details of the contact such as basic details, policy details, etc. Click the **Edit **icon on the header to edit the contact details.

The **Edit Contact **page appears to the right. Edit the contact details and click the **Update** button.

![edit contacts page](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/Edit_contact.png)

---

### All Activities

This section comprises the following tabs:

1. **Activities**: Lists the activities performed on a contact, including any deals created for the contact.
2. **Appointments**: Lists the activities related to scheduling, updating, and canceling appointments related to a contact and associated deals. If a meeting was scheduled with a contact, you can [capture the feedback](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-feedback) about the meeting by clicking the **Add Feedback** button.
3. **Calls**: Lists the activities related to voice calls made to the contact. After the call is successful, you can access the recording of the call and [add feedback](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-feedback).
4. **Tasks**: Lists the [tasks](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-tasks) associated with the contact.

#### 

### Deals

This tab displays the deals that are associated with this contact.

### Documents

This tab displays all the documents related to the contact. You can upload additional documents by clicking the **Add Document** icon. See [Work with documents](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-documents) to learn about the operations that you can perform with the documents.

---

### Notes

This section allows you to add sticky notes about the contact. You can also view the notes that are added by other users. See [Work with Notes](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/work-with-notes) to learn about the operations that you can perform with notes.
