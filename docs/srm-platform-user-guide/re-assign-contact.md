# Reassign Contacts

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/re-assign-contact>

To re-assign a contact:

1. On the [Contact List](/articles/srm-platform-user-guide/srm-platform-ui/a/h4_1409235390) page, select the checkbox of the contacts that you want to re-assign.
2. Click the** Re-assign Contacts **icon.
3. In the Re-assign Contact page, enter the following:
  1. **Total contacts selected for re-assignment: **Displays the count of Total Contacts you’ve selected to be reassigned.
  2. **User**: The user you want to assign the contacts to.
4. Click **Re-Assign**.

| ![Information](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/info.png) | For all re-assigned contacts, The new user will be the owner of the contact and associated deals. Any active tasks will be deleted. 3. Any scheduled appointments will be canceled. |
