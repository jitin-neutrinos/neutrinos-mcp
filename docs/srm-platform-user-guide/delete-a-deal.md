# Delete a Deal

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!srm-platform-user-guide/delete-a-deal>

To delete a deal, perform the following steps:

Open the deal that you want to delete.


 Click the **Delete **icon on the deal header. A pop-up appears to confirm your action.


 ![delete icon for the deals](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/delete%20icon%20for%20deals.png)


 Click **Yes **and the deal gets deleted.


 ![pop-up to confirm deletion of deal](/resources/Storage/srm-platform-user-guide/delete%20_deal_popup.png)
