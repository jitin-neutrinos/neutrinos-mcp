# Search Functionality

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!reels-publication/product-list>

The Product List page enables you to view existing products and add a new product. You can access the product list page by navigating to *Product* > *Product List*.

![](/resources/Storage/reels-publication/new-topic-4/Prodlist.png)

Product list is displayed with the following columns:

- **Product ID**: Displays the Product ID.

- **Product Name**: Displays the Product Name.
- **Product Version**: Provides Information related to different versions of a product.
- **Created Date**: The date and time of when the product was created.
- **Product Status**: Displays the product status (whether the product is deployed or in pending status).
- **Actions**: Click on the icons to perform different tasks (Edit, Delete, Copy).

**Note**: You can also sort the columns in ascending or descending order.

### Search Functionality

The search field at the top of the Product List page allows you to search for a specific product by name instead of scrolling down the list to find it.

![](/resources/Storage/reels-publication/new-topic-4/Search.png)

**Filter**

Use the filter feature to filter products by product id and product status (pending or deployed).

![](/resources/Storage/reels-publication/product-list/Filter.png)

### Pagination

Use the pagination tool at the bottom right corner of the screen to navigate through different pages and view other products. View products in sets of 5, 10,15 or 20 on a single page, based on your selection.

![](/resources/Storage/reels-publication/new-topic-4/Pagination.png)
