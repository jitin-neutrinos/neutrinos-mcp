What is a Product??

A "Product" is the result of following specific rules in a structured manner. It involves the implementation of a series of guidelines within a planned sequence, guaranteeing that each stage of the process aligns with specific pre-established requirements and limitations.

Example: **Health insurance policy as a product** - It is a product offered by insurance companies that provides financial coverage for medical and surgical expenses incurred by the insured customer. It is a contractual agreement between the insured individual and the insurance company, where the policyholder pays a premium in exchange for the insurer's promise to cover specific medical costs as outlined in the policy.

The insurance premium for the policy is determined based on factors such as the insured person's age, health history and coverage plan.
