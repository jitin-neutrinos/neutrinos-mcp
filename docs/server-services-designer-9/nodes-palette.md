## Introduction

Nodes are the key building blocks of a workflow. They perform a range of actions, including:

- Starting the workflow.
- Fetching and sending data.
- Processing and manipulating data.

## Value Addition

- Potentially endless amount of tasks can be completed by a finite amount resources.
- Separate resources into nodes and allocate tasks more effectively.
- Highly customizable, allowing users to configure to their specific needs.
