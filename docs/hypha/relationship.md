# Types of Relationships

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!hypha/relationship>

A relationship refers to the logical or semantic connection between data elements, entities, or objects across different data sources, domains, or systems. These relationships help understand how data is linked, even when the data resides in disparate locations, formats, or technologies.

# Types of Relationships

| **Type** | **Example** |
| --- | --- |
| One-to-One | A vehicle has one registration number. |
| One-to-Many | A customer has many policies. |
