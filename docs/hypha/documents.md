# Add Documents

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!hypha/documents>

In Hypha , Documents refer to files or attachments that can be linked to a specific record within an object. These documents may include PDFs, images, spreadsheets, text files, or any other supporting material required for the record.

They help users store and manage supplemental information directly alongside the record data, ensuring all context-related files remain accessible within the system.

Documents are treated as a type of related entity connected to a record—similar to how other objects are associated through relationships:

- Each record can have multiple documents associated with it (e.g., invoices for an Order record, resumes for a Candidate record, or contracts for a Client record).
- These documents appear under the Document section of a record, just like other related objects (such as Leads, Contacts, or Activities).

![hypha-object-related-objects-documents](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-documents.png)

## Add Documents

To add documents for a record, follow the steps below:

- Navigate to the object, open the required record, and go to the Documents tab from the record’s details page.
- The Documents tab lists all the documents associated with the selected record.
    ![hypha-object-related-objects-documents](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-documents.png)
- You can upload files by either dragging and dropping them or browsing your local computer. The maximum supported file size is **50 MB**, and the supported file formats include **PDF**, **JPG**, **PNG**, **JPEG**, and **DOCX**. Once the upload is successful, the document appears in the **Documents** tab for the record.

## Delete Documents

To delete documents for a record, follow the steps below:

- Navigate to the object, open the required record, and go to the Documents tab from the record’s details page.
- The Documents tab lists all the documents associated with the selected record.
    ![hypha-object-related-objects-documents](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-documents.png)
- To delete a document, click the Trash icon in the corresponding row of the document you want to remove.
   ![hypha-object-related-objects-documents-delete](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-documents-delete.png)

Additionally, you can download documents from the platform to your local system by clicking the **Download** icon in the corresponding row of the document you want to download.



![hypha-object-related-objects-documents-download](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-documents-download.png)
