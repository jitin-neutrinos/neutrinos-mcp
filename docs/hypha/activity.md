# Example Scenario

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!hypha/activity>

Activities represent the chronological log of interactions, events, or system-driven actions associated with a specific record in an object. They provide visibility into how a record has been used, updated, or acted upon over time, helping users track progress and maintain a complete audit trail.

Activities help users understand the lifecycle of a record by capturing key events such as field updates, workflow steps, user actions, communications, or system-generated operations. They enhance transparency and support decision-making, especially in process-driven or collaborative environments.

## Example Scenario

For a Support Ticket object, activities may include entries such as “Ticket created,” “Status changed from Open to In Progress,” “Assigned to Support Team,” “Resolution added,” and “Ticket closed.” These events help users understand the entire lifecycle of the ticket.

To view all the activities for a record, follow the steps below:

- Navigate to the object, open the required record, and go to the Activity tab from the record’s details page.
- The Activity page displays all actions performed on the record in a chronological timeline.
    ![hypha-object-related-objects-activity-landing-page](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-activity-landing-page.png)
- The tab allows you to filter activities based on the objects involved and the users who performed specific actions on the record. To apply a filter, click the Filter button at the top of the tab and select the required criteria. Then click Apply Filter in the prompt to apply the filter and view the filtered activity results.
    ![hypha-object-related-objects-activity-filter](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-activity-filter.png)
- After defining the filter criteria, click the Apply Filter button to apply the selected filters and refine the activities displayed for the record in the Activity tab.
    ![hypha-object-related-objects-activity-filter-criteria](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-activity-filter-criteria.png)
- Observe that, after the filter is applied, all activities related to the selected object type are displayed. In this illustration, the filter is applied to display activities only for the Lead object.
   ![hypha-object-related-objects-activity-filter-applied](/resources/Storage/hypha/images/hypha-object-related-objects-activity-filter-applied.png)
