# Step 1: Sign up with Neutrinos

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!build-an-app-on-neutrinos-platform/sign-up>

To sign up for a free trial account, perform the following steps:

1. Goto the [Neutrinos website](https://www.neutrinos.co/) and click the **Free Trial **link.
    ![free trial](/resources/Storage/build-an-app-on-neutrinos-platform/free_trial_website.png)
2. On the free trial page, enter the required metadata to create your account and click **Submit**.
    ![metadata for the sign in](/resources/Storage/build-an-app-on-neutrinos-platform/free_trial.png)
3. After submitting the data, the Neutrinos team will reach out to you. A new trial account will be set up for your organization, and you will be granted the organisation admins privilege.
