# Workbench Glossary

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!pulse-publication/workbench-glossary-wb-cm>

The terms used in this documentation are defined in the Glossary below:

- **BPM (Business Process Management)**
    A system for designing, automating, and optimizing workflows or processes. In this context, BPM manages the creation and deployment of processes.
- **BPMN 2.0**
    Business Process Model and Notation 2.0 (BPMN 2.0) standard is a globally recognized graphical notation for representing business processes in a standardized and user-friendly manner.
- **Case Instance**
    A case instance refers to a specific occurrence of a case, representing a single unit of work or business process. Each case instance is a runtime representation of a case definition.
- **Case Management**
    Case Management refers to a structured approach for managing and tracking Cases Instances throughout their lifecycle in compliance with business objectives.
- **Case Type**
    A predefined structure that associates a specific process with a case type in Case Manager (CM), establishing standards for cases that follow the same workflow.
- **Check In** and **Check Out**
    Check-In and Check-Out mechanisms manage task ownership and status within business processes, ensuring clarity on tasks actively being worked on.
- **cid (Case Identifier)**
    A unique identifier assigned to each case, enabling its reference across the system for updates, status changes, and other interactions.
- **CM (Case Manager)**
    The system responsible for managing and tracking cases, where each case represents a task or instance of a process. Case Management (CM) also stores and updates case-related information, including comments and linked documents.
- **CMIS**
    [CMIS](https://docs.oasis-open.org/cmis/CMIS/v1.1/CMIS-v1.1.html) is an open standard maintained by OASIS, enabling seamless communication between different content repositories and applications. It refers to the platform's integration capabilities for managing and interacting with enterprise content stored in various Content Management Systems (CMS) that support the CMIS standard.
- **CMS**
    CMS (Content Management System) refers to managing content including creating, updating, and removing items through dynamic content rendering.
- **co (Case Object)**
    Refers to a central data structure used to represent and track individual instances of a process or task within the system. Each case object encapsulates all the relevant data, actions, and states associated with a specific case, providing a structured way to manage and interact with process instances.
- **Dashboard**
    An interactive component visually representing the information related to cases and tasks.
- **Data Modeling**
    Data modeling refers to the process of defining, organizing, and structuring business entities, their relationships, and attributes.
- **Decision**
    Decisions are conditional steps within business processes that guide the flow based on specific criteria or user inputs. They represent branching logic, allowing processes to adapt dynamically to different scenarios by directing tasks to the appropriate next steps.
- **DMS (Document Management System)**
    A system for organizing and managing documents, where files are associated with cases to serve as reference materials or supporting documents.
- **Enquiry**
    A type of Inbox in the Neutrinos Alpha Workbench that handles internal and external ad-hoc enquiry requests.
- **Group**
    Groups refer to logical collections of users or tasks organized based on common roles and responsibilities defined by business processes.
- **Human Tasks**
    Human Tasks refer to tasks within a workflow that require manual intervention or decision-making by a human user. These tasks are part of a larger business process, where automation handles certain aspects, but specific steps need human input for validation, review, or execution.
- **IDS**
    The Identity Server is responsible for managing authentication, authorization, and user identity management.
- **Manager**
    Managers are users responsible for monitoring their reportees and tracking task progress.
- **Marketplace**
    The Marketplace refers to an ecosystem where developers, businesses, and organizations can access, discover, and integrate ready-to-use applications, components, services, and modules designed to extend the functionality of the Neutrinos Alpha platform.
- **Process Definition**
    Process Definition refers to the template for a business process that outlines its structure, tasks, rules, and workflows.
- **Process Instance**
    Process instance refers to a runtime execution of a process definition. It represents the status of the business process at any given instance.
- **Renderer Components**
    Renderer Components are the user interface (UI) components that are responsible for presenting and displaying data to users in a visually appealing and interactive way.
- **Reportees**
    Reportees are users who report to a senior user based on the roles they hold within the logical hierarchy.
- **Services**
    Services refer to modular, reusable, and configurable components that handle various tasks, processes, and integrations within the platform.
- **Task Distributor**
    Task Distributor helps distribute and manage tasks across various users within the Neutrinos Alpha platform.
- **Task Instance**
    A task instance refers to a specific task that is part of a case or business process. It represents a single actionable item or step within the process lifecycle, assigned to users, or groups for execution.
