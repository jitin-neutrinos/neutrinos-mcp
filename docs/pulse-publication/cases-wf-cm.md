# Cases

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!pulse-publication/cases-wf-cm>

A Case in Neutrinos Alpha Platform is a structured way to organize and handle information and tasks related to a specific business workflow, such as processing a claim, or issuing a new policy, and so on. It acts as a central point where data, human tasks, and services converge to complete to help you complete the work effectively.

This topic discusses the concepts and how to work on **Cases **in Workflow Studio.
