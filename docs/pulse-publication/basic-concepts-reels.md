# Basic Concepts

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!pulse-publication/basic-concepts-reels>

1. **Rule**: It is the fundamental unit of Reels, representing a single business entity that determines the outcome of a business event based on incoming data. Rules encapsulate specific logic, driving decisions across various business scenarios. For example, in the insurance sector, a rule might determine whether a customer qualifies for a discount based on their driving history. If the customer has had no accidents in the last five years, a 10% discount is applied.
2. **Rule Group**: A Rule Group is a collection of rules executed sequentially or in parallel to determine a combined outcome. It enables layered decision-making by linking multiple rules, enhancing both complexity and precision in results. For example, a Rule Group could assess a customer’s eligibility for multiple discounts, such as a no-claim bonus, loyalty discount, and safe driver discount. Each rule is evaluated, and a cumulative discount is calculated.
3. **Workflow**: A workflow integrates Rules and Rule Groups into a cohesive sequence, creating logical flows that model entire business processes. Workflows automate end-to-end scenarios by linking decisions and actions, enabling full business process automation. For example, an end-to-end claims adjudication process may involve claim eligibility rules, risk assessment rules, and fraud control rules or Rule Groups.
4. **Master Data**: Enables users to create, manage, and maintain critical business data essential for rule execution. Users can seamlessly control the data lifecycle, ensuring accurate consumption and updates within Rules, Rule Groups, and Workflows. For example, Master Data may include customer information such as policy details, driving history, and contact information. This data is used across various rules and workflows to ensure accurate processing.
