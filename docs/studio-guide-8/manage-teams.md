# Create a Team

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!studio-guide-8/manage-teams>

When an organisation admins gets access to the Neutrinos Platform, Neutrinos creates an **Organization** for the organisation admins on Neutrinos Console. The organisation admins create users for his/her organization to provide authentication and creates **Teams** to provide authorization to applications and Neutrinos products such as Console, Studio, etc.

### Create a Team

To create a Team:

- Log in to the Neutrinos console and click T**eams**.

- Click ![the Add Team icon](/resources/Storage/studio-guide-8/add_team.png) on the top right of the screen.
- Add the following information and click ![the Edit icon](/resources/Storage/studio-guide-8/correct.png) :

![Adding Teams on Console](/resources/Storage/studio-guide-8/team_console.png)

When a team is created, the users who were added to the team will be provided access to the selected Neutrinos products and the applications created on Neutrinos Studio. Users can use their username/email and password to access these resources.

### Update a Team

To update a team, select the **Team** that you want to update. The existing information about the team is displayed to the right. Update the necessary information and click ![the Update Team icon](/resources/Storage/studio-guide-8/correct.png).

You can use the search bar to perform a search based on the team name.

### Delete a Team

To delete a team, select the **Team** that you want to delete. The existing information about the team is displayed to the right. Click ![the Delete icon](/resources/Storage/studio-guide-8/delete_icon.png) to remove the team from your organization.

You can use the search bar to perform a search based on the team name.
