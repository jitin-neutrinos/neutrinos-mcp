# Add Group

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!alpha-platform/groups>

A **Group** is a way to organize two of the most important aspects of the Neutrinos Alpha Platform: Users and Tasks. It serves as a logical unit that allows administrators to retrieve sets of tasks or users. Users can be grouped logically by roles, such as Underwriter, Operations, Developer, Business User, or Manager, or by responsibilities, such as Senior Underwriting Reviewer, Operations, Underwriting, Investigation, or Documentation. Task groups, on the other hand, facilitate automated task distribution. The image below showcases the **Groups** page in Admin UI. The group page shows the **Group Names**, **Description** of respective groups, number of **Users** in the group, and the date on which the group was **Updated**.

![group in Admin UI](/resources/Storage/alpha-platform/images/group-adminui.png)

The key purpose of groups in Neutrinos Alpha:

1. **User Management**: Groups help in managing users by organizing them based on roles, tasks, or departments. This makes it easier to assign permissions and manage access collectively rather than individually.
2. **Tasks Allocation**: By organizing users into groups, tasks can be allocated more effectively, ensuring that each group is assigned tasks suited to their skills, without unnecessary overlap or conflict.
3. **Collaboration**: Groups facilitate better collaboration by allowing members to share resources, data, and insights within a controlled environment. This enhances productivity and ensures that team members are aligned.
4. **Scalability**: As businesses grow, managing a large number of users through groups becomes more manageable and less prone to errors.
5. **Auditing and Compliance**: Grouping users simplifies auditing processes by providing clear records of who has access to what, which is crucial for compliance and security purposes.

| ![Note](/resources/Storage/alpha-platform/note.png) | An Admin can update the group name and description by clicking the kebab icon located in the **Actions** column for the respective group entry. Additionally, the Admin can delete the group from the same option. |
| --- | --- |

To summarize, groups in Neutrinos Alpha provide a better way for managing users, permissions, and tasks, enhancing collaboration and ensuring efficient administration within the platform.

## Add Group

Admins can create new logical groups based on the business process requirements. To create a new group, follow these steps:

1. Click the **Add Group** button located in the top-right corner of the **Groups** page.
2. In the **Add Group **page that opens, enter the relevant **Group Name** and** Description**.
3. Click **Add Group **button in the bottom of the page to finalize the the group creation. After the group is successfully created, a success toast message will appear.
