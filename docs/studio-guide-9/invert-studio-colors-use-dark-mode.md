# Invert Studio Colors (Use Dark Mode)

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!studio-guide-9/invert-studio-colors-use-dark-mode>

An experimental **Dark**** Mode **theme is added to Studio to provide visual ergonomics.  The dark theme reduces the strain on the eyes by adjusting the brightness to current lighting conditions and conserves battery power.

| ![Information](/resources/Storage/studio-guide-9/info.png) | This theme is currently experimental. You may encounter inconsistencies in some User Interface elements. The images and icons in the **Assets** folder might also look different. |
| --- | --- |

Click **View > Invert Colors (Experimental Dark Mode)** on the top menu to switch the Studio to dark mode. To switch back to the original theme, click **View > Invert Colors (Experimental Dark Mode) **again.

Once you choose a theme, it is maintained across Studio till it restarts, or until the $Home/.neutrinos folder is deleted from your local machine.
