# Deploy and Preview the App

<https://documentation.neutrinos.com/articles/#!tutorial-create-your-first-app/deploy-and-preview-the-apps>

**Deploy App**

| ![Information](/resources/Storage/tutorial-create-your-first-app/project-tutorial-weather-app/info.png) | The **Deploy App** feature is enabled for users having access (clients) to Neutrinos Console. If you have access to Neutrinos Console and are unable to use the Deploy App feature, contact your organization admin or reach out to [Neutrinos Support](mailto:freetrial@neutrinos.co). |
| --- | --- |

To deploy an app on the cloud:

1. Click on the Deploy app ![](/resources/Storage/tutorial-create-your-first-app/project-tutorial-weather-app/understanding-the-application-home-page-2019-04-16-5.png) icon on the top-right corner of the app editor screen.

2. Select the deployment targets for the build.

3. Click the **Deploy** button to schedule the build for deployment.

![Deploy LMS app](/resources/Storage/tutorial-create-your-first-app/project-tutorial-weather-app/deploy.png)

To check the status of your deployment, login to [Neutrinos Console](https://console.neutrinos.co/). Navigate to **apps** section and select your app card and view build status updates.

![Weather app deployed](/resources/Storage/tutorial-create-your-first-app/weather_deployed.png)

The app deployment process involves multiple steps that require a couple of minutes to complete. You can view individual steps status in the **app build details** section.

![Weather app build status](/resources/Storage/tutorial-create-your-first-app/weather_build.png)

When your app build has completed successfully, the build status is updated to **Success**.

| ![Information](/resources/Storage/tutorial-create-your-first-app/project-tutorial-weather-app/info.png) | In the case of Build Failure, download Build logs and check for app errors.If you face any connectivity issues during the deployment steps, you can use the** Re-deploy **option on the app card to re-schedule a build for deployment.You can also use the **Deploy app** option on Studio to redeploy app changes onto the cloud. |
| --- | --- |

**Preview App**

When your build is completed successfully, click the Portal ![](/resources/Storage/tutorial-create-your-first-app/project-tutorial-weather-app/portal_icon.png) icon to launch the web app or click the **Android/iOS** icon to download the android build or iOS build of the app.
