## Image Gallery

Overview




 The photo gallery application is compatible with literally any website or blog.


 This template has Neutrinos IDS OAuth Strategy enabled. See [Configure IDS](/smart/project-sample-how-to-guide/configure-your-ids) to learn more.




 **Usage**




 There are some other popular usages of the gallery template. Some of them are:







 Personalized recommendations-user-based






 Most popular paintings on a museum website






 Landscape photos in traveling blog






 Popular Items and User-defined ranking






 Business rules

### How to Use

1. In the Neutrinos Store, open the **Image Gallery** template.
2. Click the **Download** icon and select **Open Neutrinos Studio** in the pop-up that appears and you will be navigated to the Neutrinos Studio.
3. In the Neutrinos Studio, click **Yes** on the confirmation pop-up and the template gets installed.
4. Create a new app using the image-gallery template and enter the metadata for your app in the **ENTER APP DETAILS** window. Click **CREATE** and the app gets created.
5. Open the created app and navigate to the** Settings** menu. By default, the Neutrinos IDS OAuth Strategy is selected. Enter the **Application display name** and click **Save. **
6. You will see a pop-up to confirm registering for the IDS. Click **Okay** and the IDS flows are generated.
7. Navigate to the server services editor and confirm whether the IDS flows are generated. If the flows are not generated, regenerate the flows in the IDS settings editor.
8. If the flows are not generated, navigate back to the IDS settings editor and click the **Regenerate IDS Flows **button.
9. After confirming, **Initialize** your app.
10. After initialization is completed. Select **Live View** and run the task.
11. The template is rendered on the browser. Login to the template and the template looks like this.

![First page of the template](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/IIGF1.png) ![Final template page](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/IGF2.png)

You can further design the template as per your needs in the application.
