## Dashboard

Overview



 Dashboards belong to the most advanced UI category. They are designed for organization and visualization of the huge amount of data and for managing multiple functionalities of the application.


 **This template has Neutrinos IDS OAuth Strategy enabled. See [Configure IDS](/smart/project-sample-how-to-guide/configure-your-ids) to learn more.


 **


 **Usage**






 The dashboard is a reporting and monitoring tool used to visually depict the performance of an organization, department, or project. There are some other popular usages of the dashboard. Some of them are:








 Sales Dashboards






 Marketing Dashboards






 A Social Media Dashboard





 How to Use



 In the Neutrinos Store, open the **Dashboard** template.


 Click the **Download** icon and select **Open Neutrinos Studio** in the pop-up that appears and you will be navigated to the Neutrinos Studio.


 ![open Neutrinos Studio](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/DSH%21.png)




 In the Neutrinos Studio, click **Yes** on the confirmation pop-up and the template gets installed.


 ![confirmation popup](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/DSH2.png)




 Create a new app using the dashboard template and enter the metadata for your app in the **ENTER APP DETAILS** window. Click **CREATE** and the app gets created.


 ![Create app with template](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/DSH3.png)




 Open the created app and navigate to the** Settings** menu. By default, the Neutrinos IDS OAuth Strategy is selected. Enter the **Application display name** and click **Save. **


 ![Generate IDS flows](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/IC4.png)




 You will see a pop-up to confirm registering for the IDS. Click **Okay** and the IDS flows are generated.


 ![Confirm to generate IDS flows](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/IDSConfirm.png)




 Navigate to the server services editor and confirm whether the IDS flows are generated. If the flows are not generated, regenerate the flows in the IDS settings editor.


 ![server services of the template](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/serverservicesTEMP.png)




 If the flows are not generated, navigate back to the IDS settings editor and click the **Regenerate IDS Flows **button.


 ![server services of the template](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/regen.png)




 After confirming, **Initialize** your app.


 After initialization is completed. Select **Live View** and run the task.


 The template is rendered on the browser. Login to the template and the template looks like this.


 ![First page of the template](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/DSHF1.png) ![dashboard final image](/resources/Storage/components-guide-for-release-6/DSHF2.png)


 You can further design the template as per your needs in the application.
