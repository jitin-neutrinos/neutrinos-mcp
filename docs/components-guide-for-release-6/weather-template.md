## Weather Template

### Overview

This template is a weather application that displays the weather status of any city that the user will input on the runtime.

### How to Use

1. In the Neutrinos Store, open the **Weather** template.
2. Click the **Download** icon and select **Open Neutrinos Studio** in the pop-up that appears and you will be navigated to the Neutrinos Studio.
3. In the Neutrinos Studio, click **Yes** on the confirmation pop-up and the template gets installed.
4. Create a new app using the weather template and enter the metadata for your app in the **ENTER APP DETAILS** window. Click **CREATE** and the app gets created.
5. Open the created app and **Initialize** your app.
6. After initialization is completed. Select **Live View** and run the task.
7. The template is rendered on the browser. Login to the template and the template looks like this.

You can further design the template as per your needs in the application.
